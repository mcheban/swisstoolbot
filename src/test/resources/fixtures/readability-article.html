The Readability Article

The quick brown fox jumps over the lazy dog while the diligent hamster stores sunflower seeds in its cheek pouches for the long and cold winter ahead.

Readability keeps the paragraphs that carry the story together with their bold and italic flourishes, such as this emphasised phrase and this softer aside, and it drops the surrounding chrome like navigation bars and footers.

Because the algorithm weighs text density and punctuation, a handful of well formed paragraphs is usually enough for it to pick the right container without any hints.
